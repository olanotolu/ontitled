Frequently asked

questions

What is Themis by Ontitled?

Themis or our's creative RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) uses professional creatives—like designers, writers, and marketers—to evaluate and fine-tune AI model outputs based on subjective quality, style, and brand fit. [Ontitled] is setting a new standard with the Human Creativity Benchmark, helping to shape the future of creative tools.

How does [Ontitled] help AI companies train creative models?

[Ontitled] connects AI teams with real creative professionals who review and rank model outputs. This human feedback improves how models generate copy, images, videos, and designs that align with real-world creative standards.

Who are the reviewers in the Themis network?

[Ontitled]’s reviewers are vetted creative professionals with real client experience. They’ve worked on branding, marketing, product design, and more. We match them to tasks based on style, skill, and audience relevance. This is not crowd sourced labor. These are top tier creative professionals already earning a living doing what they do best. Meet them where they are on the commission-free creative network.

What types of model outputs are a good fit for Themis?

You can use [Ontitled] to evaluate and fine-tune text, images, videos, audio, user flows, and multi-modal creative outputs. Our reviewers give feedback on clarity, tone, emotion, storytelling, and overall creative quality.

Is Themis right for my team?

If you’re building generative tools for design, writing, video, or marketing, [Ontitled] helps you train models that understand creative nuance—so your outputs feel more human, brand-safe, and audience-ready.
